# WindWatch 0.0.6

## New

- **Gusts are easier to read:** the wind now shows as `12 G18 kn`, a mean wind of 12 knots with gusts of 18, as in sailing and aviation weather reports. The old `12-18` looked like a range of possible speeds.
- **Choose the wind format** in the app settings: `12 G18`, `12 (18)` or `12-18`.
- **More units:** m/s, mph and Beaufort join knots and km/h in the app settings.
- **Unit and wind format on the watch:** the menu behind START now also switches them, without the phone.
- **Wind stays on screen without a connection:** the last known wind and forecast now stay on screen when the phone is offline or in airplane mode. A mark behind the wind says when they are not the current ones:
  - a **pin** when they are for another position, like after you moved on in GPS mode
  - a **clock** when they are older than 30 minutes, as a refresh failed

## Fixed

- In GPS mode the screen went blank without a connection as soon as you moved about a kilometer.
